我是谁Who I am

01

自学型Self-taught

入学前只会一点 C，没碰过硬件。我没有等课上学，而是自己排了一条路线：电路基础 → STM32 外设 → 控制算法，按周推进。 I arrived with a little C and no hardware. Instead of waiting for a course, I laid out my own route: circuits → STM32 peripherals → control algorithms, advanced weekly.

02

数据控Data-first

我不满足于「Kp 大了会超调」这种话。我写了一个仿真，扫了 18 组参数，把上升时间、超调、稳态误差全部量化成 CSV——现在我能指着数据说清每一项。 “More Kp means more overshoot” isn't enough for me. I wrote a simulation, swept 18 parameter sets, and turned rise time, overshoot and steady-state error into CSV — now I can point at data for every claim.

03

不装No bluffing

没上过机就写「待上机」，不会 CAN 就写「还没接触」。把不会的写在最显眼的地方，比被面试官问出来强得多。 Never touched a board → I write “pending hardware”. Never written CAN → I write “not yet”. Putting the gaps in plain sight beats being caught out by an interviewer.
